What it is

More buyers now ask an AI assistant before they open a search results page. AI search visibility, sometimes called GEO or AEO, is the work of making your brand clear, quotable and trusted so assistants include you in their answers.

What you get

  • Question led content with clear, quotable answers
  • FAQ sections and structured data on key pages
  • Review of how assistants describe your brand today
  • Brand mentions on the sources assistants read
  • Ongoing checks as the tools change

How it works

Three steps, clear owners.

01Check

We ask the questions your buyers ask and record who gets mentioned.

02Improve

We rewrite and structure your pages so answers are easy to find and cite.

03Track

We repeat the checks and report what has changed.

Questions

About ai search visibility

Is this different from SEO?

They overlap. Good SEO helps, but AI assistants also favour clear answers, consistent facts and trusted mentions across the web, so there is extra work involved.

Can you guarantee a mention?

No one can. We improve the signals assistants use and track the result openly.
