What it is

A funnel is the path a visitor takes from first click to becoming a lead or customer. We design and build that path end to end: the offer, the landing page, the follow up emails and the tracking, so you can see exactly where people drop off.

What you get

  • Offer and lead magnet planning
  • Landing and thank you pages
  • Email follow up sequences
  • Connections between your website, email tool and CRM
  • Tracking and reporting at every step of the funnel

How it works

Three steps, clear owners.

01

Map

We agree the goal, the audience and the path from first click to lead.

02

Build

We create the pages, offers and emails, then connect the tools.

03

Measure

We track each step, find the leaks and improve the weakest one first.

What is a lead magnet?

A free resource, such as a report, a checklist or a webinar, that someone receives in exchange for their contact details.

Which tools do you build in?

We build in the tools you already use, such as ActiveCampaign or Mailchimp, and connect them to your website and CRM.
