About

A small team that shows up.

Tan Media is a UK digital marketing company. We give businesses a dependable marketing team that plans carefully, delivers every week and reports honestly.

Why we exist

Most small and mid sized businesses need marketing from several specialists but cannot hire them all. Tan Media brings those skills together in one team, so search, paid, content and email are planned side by side.

We keep the team small on purpose. The person who plans your work also does it, and you always know who to ask.

What we believe

  • Plain language beats jargon.
  • A goal that cannot be measured is not yet a goal.
  • Retainers should be earned, not assumed.
  • Honest reporting builds longer relationships than good news does.
